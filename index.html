The Practice

At the heart of Wabis Work is an invitation, to create ritual, and live intention. The simple action of turning on a light becomes an opportunity, to pause, to take a breath, to be present. In this moment, set an intention, and as the light brings illumination, let your intention radiate into your space.

The Process

Small-batch craft. Never mass produced.

Built with heart, by a single pair of hands, from beginning to end.

Like you, I believe functional art should nourish the soul while serving daily life. Grown from a deep respect for natural materials, intentional design, and the imperfect beauty of wabi-sabi, I build pieces meant to be enjoyed for generations.

“I put my hands to work knowing the task, seeing time and intention bloom into something genuinely beautiful.”

Robert Furin, founder of Wabis Work

The Product

Each piece is an offering of functional beauty: sculpture and utility in one object. Every use is an opportunity to fill your room with light and intention. Crafted with an attention to detail meant to be enjoyed over a lifetime.

  • 2200K Edison bulb in amber glass
  • Finished with boiled linseed oil
  • Dimmer for adjustable ambient glow
  • Offered in solid ash, alder or walnut
  • Choice of black, antique brass or polished brass chain
